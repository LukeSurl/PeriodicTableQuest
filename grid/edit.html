      Generate questions from lecture material with AI prompt ChatGPT, Copilot or similar to draft questions for you
      
This gives you a ready-made request (a "prompt") to paste into an AI chat, together with your lecture material. Your AI is directed to reply with questions in exactly the format this page can read, so you can add them all in one go.

      


      
    
        
  1. Choose what you want.
          
    
            
            
            
            
            
          
    
        
    2. 
        
  2. Copy the prompt.  
          
        
    3. 
        
  3. Open your AI chat tool and start a new chat. Click in the message box and paste (Ctrl+V, or ⌘+V on a Mac).
    4. 
        
  4. Add your lecture material to the same message. Attach your slides or notes with the + or 📎 paperclip button next to the message box (PowerPoint and PDF files both work). If you can't attach a file, paste the text of your slides underneath the prompt instead. Then send the message.
    5. 
        
  5. Copy the AI's reply. For most AIs the questions come back in a shaded box. Click the Copy button at the top or bottom of that box. (Copying the whole reply also works: any extra chat is ignored.)
    6. 
        
  6. Paste the reply here.
          
          
    
          
    This opens the import box above, which checks every question before you add them.
    
        
    7. 
        
  7. Read every question before you use it. AI makes mistakes: check each correct answer, and that the wrong answers really are wrong. You can edit any question after adding it, and add pictures.
