Quiz Grid

A pre-lecture team quiz. You write a handful of multiple-choice questions; the screen shows them as a grid. Students scan a QR code, are put into one of two teams, and answer the questions on their phones. Every correct answer wins a point for the student's team; each student can score once per question, so the more of a team who get each question right, the better. The team with the most points when the timer ends wins.
