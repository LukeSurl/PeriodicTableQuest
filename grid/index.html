Pre-Lecture Quizzes

Quiz Grid

A pre-lecture team quiz. You write a handful of multiple-choice questions; the screen shows them as a grid. Students scan a QR code, are put into one of two teams, and answer the questions on their phones. The team with more correct answers on a question wins that question, and the team that wins the most questions wins the game (total correct answers break a tie). The team with the most points when the timer ends wins.
