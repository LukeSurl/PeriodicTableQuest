Wavelength and frequency are inversely proportional for electromagnetic radiation. Why?
- Longer waves carry more energy
* All electromagnetic radiation travels at the same speed, c
- Photons slow down as frequency rises
- Frequency and wavelength are measured in the same units
# Explanation: c = λν with c fixed, so if more waves pass each second (higher ν), each wave must be shorter (smaller λ).

Which list is in order of increasing energy per photon?
* Radio, infrared, visible, ultraviolet
- Ultraviolet, visible, infrared, radio
- Visible, radio, ultraviolet, infrared
- Infrared, radio, visible, ultraviolet
# Explanation: Energy per photon rises with frequency (E = hν), so it rises as wavelength falls: radio < microwave < infrared < visible < UV < X-rays.

NMR spectroscopy uses radio waves. What transitions does it involve?
* Between nuclear spin levels in a magnetic field
- Between electronic energy levels
- Between molecular vibrational states
- Ejection of core electrons
# Explanation: Radio photons have very little energy, enough only for the tiny gaps between nuclear spin levels.

UV-visible spectroscopy probes transitions between…
- nuclear spin levels
- vibrational states
* electronic energy levels
- rotational states
# Explanation: UV and visible photons have enough energy to move electrons between energy levels, which is also why atoms give coloured flames.
