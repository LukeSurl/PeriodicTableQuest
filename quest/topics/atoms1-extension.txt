Radiocarbon dating measures how much of which isotope is left in a sample?
Elements: C
- ¹²C
- ¹³C
* ¹⁴C
- ¹¹C
# Explanation: Living things take in ¹⁴C with their carbon. After death it decays away with a half-life of about 5,730 years, so what remains dates the sample.

¹⁴C has a half-life of about 5,730 years. What fraction of the original ¹⁴C is left after about 11,460 years?
Elements: C
- One half
* One quarter
- None
- One eighth
# Explanation: 11,460 years is two half-lives: ½ × ½ = ¼.

In 1941 Ruben and Kamen gave plants water labelled with ¹⁸O and found the label in the O₂ they released. What did this show?
Elements: O
- The O₂ from photosynthesis comes from CO₂
- Plants absorb O₂ through their roots
* The O₂ from photosynthesis comes from water
- ¹⁸O is radioactive
# Explanation: Using a heavy but stable isotope as a tracer let them follow oxygen atoms from water to oxygen gas.

The PET tracer ¹⁸F-FDG accumulates in tissues with a high metabolic rate, such as many tumours. FDG is an analogue of…
Elements: F
- ATP
- cholesterol
- haemoglobin
* glucose
# Explanation: Fluorodeoxyglucose is taken up like glucose. Fluorine-18 has a half-life of about 110 minutes, long enough to scan but short enough to keep the dose low.

In 1913 J.J. Thomson found that neon gave two traces in his apparatus, at masses 20 and 22. This was early evidence that…
Elements: Ne
- neon is radioactive
- neon forms Ne₂ molecules
* stable elements can exist as more than one isotope
- atoms can gain protons
# Explanation: Neon was the first stable (non-radioactive) element shown to have isotopes.

In the Hershey–Chase experiment, ³²P was used to label the bacteriophage's…
Elements: P
- protein coat
- lipid membrane
* DNA
- tail fibres only
# Explanation: DNA has a phosphate backbone, while protein contains little phosphorus. The ³²P went into the bacteria, showing DNA is the genetic material.

In the Hershey–Chase experiment, ³⁵S was used to label protein. Why sulfur?
Elements: S
* Protein contains sulfur (in Cys and Met) but DNA does not
- DNA contains sulfur but protein does not
- Sulfur is the most abundant element in protein
- Sulfur binds only to DNA
# Explanation: Each label marked one kind of molecule. The ³⁵S stayed outside the bacteria with the empty phage coats.

In a mass spectrum, a molecule containing one Br atom shows peaks at M and M+2 in a ratio of roughly…
Elements: Br
- 3 : 1
- 1 : 3
- 9 : 1
* 1 : 1
# Explanation: Bromine is about 51% ⁷⁹Br and 49% ⁸¹Br, so the two peaks are almost equal in height.

Bananas are very slightly radioactive because of the potassium they contain. Which isotope is responsible?
Elements: K
- ³⁹K
- ⁴¹K
* ⁴⁰K
- ³⁸K
# Explanation: About 0.012% of natural potassium is ⁴⁰K, which has a half-life of about 1.25 billion years.

Strontium-90 from nuclear fallout builds up in bones and teeth. Why?
Elements: Sr
- ⁹⁰Sr has the same mass as calcium
- Bones absorb any radioactive element
- Strontium replaces phosphorus in DNA
* Strontium is in the same group as calcium and behaves chemically like it
# Explanation: Isotope identity sets the radioactivity; group membership sets the chemistry. The body treats Sr²⁺ much like Ca²⁺.

Technetium-99m is the most widely used isotope in medical imaging. Which statement about technetium is true?
Elements: Tc
- It is the most abundant metal in the Earth's crust
- All its isotopes have 43 neutrons
* It has no stable isotopes
- It is a noble gas
# Explanation: Technetium (Z = 43) is the lightest element with no stable isotope, which is why hospitals make it on site from a molybdenum-99 generator.

Hospitals produce technetium-99m from a "generator" containing which parent isotope?
Elements: Mo
- ⁹⁸Mo
* ⁹⁹Mo
- ⁹⁹Ru
- ¹⁰⁰Mo
# Explanation: Molybdenum-99 decays to technetium-99m. Same mass number, but Z goes up by one, from 42 to 43.

Promethium and technetium share an unusual property among the elements lighter than lead. What is it?
Elements: Pm
- They are both gases
- They have only one electron shell
- They each have exactly one isotope
* They have no stable isotopes
# Explanation: Every isotope of Tc (Z = 43) and Pm (Z = 61) is radioactive. All other elements up to lead have at least one stable isotope.

Tin holds the record for the most stable isotopes of any element. How many?
Elements: Sn
* 10
- 3
- 6
- 21
# Explanation: Tin (Z = 50) has ten stable isotopes, from ¹¹²Sn to ¹²⁴Sn.

Radioactive iodine-131 is used to treat an overactive thyroid gland. Why does it end up in the thyroid?
Elements: I
- ¹³¹I is attracted by the thyroid's magnetic field
- The thyroid absorbs any radioactive substance
* The thyroid takes up iodine to make thyroid hormones
- Iodine-131 binds only to thyroid DNA
# Explanation: The body cannot tell ¹³¹I from stable ¹²⁷I, so the radioactive isotope follows iodine's normal route.

The SI second is defined using the radiation from a transition in which atom?
Elements: Cs
* ¹³³Cs
- ¹²C
- ⁸⁶Kr
- ¹H
# Explanation: Since 1967 the second has been defined by exactly 9,192,631,770 cycles of radiation from caesium-133, the element's only stable isotope.

From 1960 to 1983 the metre was defined using light emitted by which isotope?
Elements: Kr
* ⁸⁶Kr
- ¹³³Cs
- ⁴⁰Ar
- ²⁰Ne
# Explanation: An orange-red line of krypton-86 defined the metre until it was redefined via the speed of light.

The relative atomic mass of lead varies noticeably between samples from different mines. Why?
Elements: Pb
- Lead atoms gain protons over time
- Lead is weighed differently in different countries
- Lead's electrons are easily lost
* Three of its isotopes are end products of uranium and thorium decay, so the mix depends on the rock's history
# Explanation: ²⁰⁶Pb, ²⁰⁷Pb and ²⁰⁸Pb build up from decay of U and Th, so older or uranium-rich rocks give lead with a different isotope mix.

Bismuth-209 was found in 2003 to decay, with a half-life of about 2 × 10¹⁹ years. The universe is about 1.4 × 10¹⁰ years old. The half-life is roughly…
Elements: Bi
- a thousand times the age of the universe
- about the same as the age of the universe
- a million times shorter than the age of the universe
* a billion times the age of the universe
# Explanation: 2 × 10¹⁹ ÷ 1.4 × 10¹⁰ ≈ 1.4 × 10⁹, so about a billion times longer. For all practical purposes bismuth is stable.

Parts of Devon and Cornwall have high levels of radon-222 in homes. Where does it come from?
Elements: Rn
- Car exhausts
- Sea spray
- Cosmic rays hitting the atmosphere
* Decay of uranium in granite
# Explanation: Radon-222 is part of the uranium-238 decay chain. Granite is relatively rich in uranium, and radon, being a gas, seeps into buildings.

Radium-226 (Z = 88) decays by emitting an alpha particle (a ⁴He nucleus). What does it become?
Elements: Ra
* ²²²Rn (Z = 86)
- ²²²Ra (Z = 88)
- ²²⁶Rn (Z = 86)
- ²²⁴Rn (Z = 86)
# Explanation: Losing 2 protons and 2 neutrons takes the mass number down by 4 and Z down by 2, turning radium into radon.

Natural uranium is 99.3% ²³⁸U and 0.7% ²³⁵U. When uranium is "enriched" for fuel, what changes?
Elements: U
- Extra protons are added to each atom
* The proportion of ²³⁵U increases
- The uranium is converted into plutonium
- The atoms gain electrons
# Explanation: Enrichment changes the isotope mix of the sample, not the atoms themselves.

NASA's Curiosity rover on Mars is powered by heat from the decay of which isotope?
Elements: Pu
- ²³⁵U
- ¹⁴C
* ²³⁸Pu
- ⁶⁰Co
# Explanation: Plutonium-238 has a half-life of about 88 years, giving steady heat for decades. Voyager 1 and 2 use it too.

Many household smoke alarms contain a tiny amount of which isotope?
Elements: Am
- ²²²Rn
* ²⁴¹Am
- ¹³¹I
- ⁹⁰Sr
# Explanation: Americium-241 ionises the air in a small chamber; smoke disrupts the current and triggers the alarm.
