Periodic Table Quest: demo

The projector and two student phones, running in this one page with no server. Tap any element on a phone to practise, then press Start on the projector and claim elements. Tap the other team's elements to try a steal. A few elements (such as Na, Cl and the noble gases) also have questions of their own. To try the editor, edit this demo quiz in another tab: your changes appear here as you type.
