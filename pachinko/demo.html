Pachinko: demo

The projector and two student phones, running in this one page with no server. Press Start on the projector, then tap a question on either phone. Each right answer drops a ball down the board; the zone it lands in sets the points. The last two questions are worth ×2 and ×3. To try the editor, edit this demo quiz in another tab: your changes appear here as you type.
